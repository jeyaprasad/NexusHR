Employees are entitled to 20 days of paid time off per year. Sick leave is unlimited but requires a doctor's note after 3 consecutive days.

Benefits: All full-time employees are eligible for standard health insurance (comprehensive medical, dental, and vision coverage) starting from their first day of employment. Dependents can be added during the annual open enrollment period.

Onboarding: During your first week, you will complete the standard onboarding process. This includes IT setup (laptop provisioning, account creation), mandatory compliance training, and introductory meetings with your team lead. Please ensure you have submitted your tax forms and direct deposit information to HR by day 3.

Payroll: Employees are paid on a bi-weekly cycle (every other Friday). Payslips are issued electronically on the Wednesday before payday and can be accessed via the payroll portal. If you notice any discrepancy in your pay, please raise a payroll discrepancy ticket in the internal portal immediately.
